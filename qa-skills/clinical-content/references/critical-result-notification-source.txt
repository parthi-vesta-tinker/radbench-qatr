Title: Critical Result Notification

Guidelines: Organizational policy requires verbal notification for all critical results within sixty (60) minutes.
Notification requires Vesta to make a call to the facility contact listed for critical findings, the date
and time of the notification should be noted in the Impression of the final report.

Guardrails:
Known findings will not be called unless there is a significant change - per the doctor’s judgment *

● Herniation Syndrome
● Acute Stroke
● Intracranial infection/empyema
● Complex Skull fracture
● Unstable Spine Fracture
● Spinal Cord Compression, hemorrhage or infarct

● Tension Pneumothorax
● Pneumoperitoneum/ Free Air
● Ischemic Bowel (Pneumatosis)
● Active Hemorrhage
● Cardiac Tamponade
● Volvulus/closed loop obstruction
● High Grade Traumatic Visceral Injury
● Retroperitoneal hemorrhage
● Bowel Obstruction
● Appendicitis
