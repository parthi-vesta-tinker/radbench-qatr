# Critical review and policy boundaries

Recognize clinically consequential current positive or suspected statements. Evaluate negation,
certainty, timing, qualifiers and context before deciding whether to surface a concern. Merely
mentioning a critical diagnosis in an indication, historical paragraph or negation is insufficient.
No patient's current diagnosis may be invented from a textbook association.

## Three explicit assessment bases

catalog_match: an applicable server-bound draft evaluation catalog exists and a report statement
supports a candidate entry. Preserve its qualifiers and exact policy/rule identifiers. Distinguish
explicit diagnosis, cautious semantic equivalence and descriptor-only uncertainty. Derived catalog
notes are not new policy. Do not add severity/acuity to unqualified policy entries or strip qualifiers
from qualified entries. Descriptor overlap does not prove every part of a compound diagnosis.

outside_catalog: an applicable draft evaluation catalog was supplied but the independently consequential
current statement has no adequate entry. Name what is reported and retain scope uncertainty. Do not
pretend it is a confirmed catalog match, invent a rule ID or use this route for all ordinary abnormalities.

generic_provisional: no applicable matching catalog is supplied. Apply clinical knowledge
to reported current concerns, naming their uncertainty and requesting radiologist assessment. Do not
claim Vesta-specific approval, a local policy violation, a mandated interval or a universal alert threshold.
An unvalidated manual may inform candidate review but cannot establish an unmet approved requirement.

## Separate correction from critical concern

A report inconsistency is not itself a critical condition. The critical stage must find a distinct
clinical basis in the text for every critical entry. A small effusion side conflict belongs in general
comments. A report asserting an acute PE in Findings and denying it in Impression can support a
general reconciliation and a separate potential critical concern, with the contradiction retained
as uncertainty. They have different actions and independently supported reasons to exist.

## Designation is not detection or communication

Return a critical observation even when the report explicitly says it was flagged. A current,
explicit report-level critical designation can support documented_flagged; an explicit assertion
that it was not flagged can support documented_not_flagged. Supply an exact anchor for either.
Absence, ambiguous, conflicting, hypothetical or historical flag statements yield unknown with no
anchor. A phone-call record alone does not establish a PACS flag. Diagnosis presence, absence
from Impression, and missing metadata never prove a missed flag or missed call.

A known/unchanged finding is evaluated in its actual current context. A supplied known-finding
notification exception may inform the requested action but does not erase a retained current
critical observation. Do not request a repeat call when a relevant supplied exception applies;
ask for designation/pathway confirmation as needed. Never state that communication occurred or
that an exception was satisfied without textual and policy support.

## Comments

Name the reported finding and useful radiologist action with uncertainty preserved. Keep evidence
and policy interpretation in the brief basis, not a long pasted message. No treatment instructions,
invented destination/deadline or automatic notification action. Operational steps are host guidance.
